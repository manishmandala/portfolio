AI Receptionist

An AI-powered virtual receptionist for a real estate agent's inbox and client relationships - built as a set of n8n workflows using Claude, Gmail, Supabase, and Telegram. Classifies incoming email, drafts replies in the agent's voice, nurtures past clients on a schedule, and coordinates active transactions. Every draft is reviewed by the agent before anything sends.
